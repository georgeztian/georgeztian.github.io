Research

Exploring the intersections of corporate finance, fintech, and asset pricing

Research Interests

My research focuses on the following areas:

  • Corporate Finance: Capital structure decisions, investment policies, and corporate governance
  • Fintech: Financial innovation, digital currencies, blockchain applications, and their impact on traditional finance
  • Asset Pricing: Risk factors, market efficiency, and pricing models

Research Approach

I employ empirical and theoretical methods to investigate important questions in finance. My work combines rigorous econometric analysis with careful economic intuition to provide insights into financial markets and corporate decision-making.
