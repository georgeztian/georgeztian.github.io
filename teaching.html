Teaching

I am committed to teaching excellence and providing students with rigorous training in finance fundamentals and modern financial innovations. I currently teach courses in corporate finance and financial technology at the University of Tampa.

Current Courses

Disruptive Innovations in FinTech (FIN 677) – 2026–present. Graduate-level course on financial technology, digital innovation, cryptocurrency, and blockchain applications in finance.

Financial Policies and Strategies (FIN 491) – 2025–present. Advanced undergraduate course on corporate financial strategy and policy decisions.

Intermediate Financial Management (FIN 410) – 2024–2025. Intermediate-level course covering capital budgeting, financing decisions, and dividend policy.

Financial Management (FIN 310) – Fall 2024. Introductory course in corporate financial management.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe that effective finance education combines rigorous theoretical foundations with practical applications. I aim to create engaging classroom environments where students develop both analytical skills and critical thinking abilities. My courses emphasize real-world relevance, quantitative rigor, and the importance of ethical decision-making in finance.

Academic Experience

Beyond my current role at the University of Tampa, I have teaching experience as both instructor and teaching assistant at the University of Houston and Oregon State University, covering courses in investment management, valuation, energy trading, and statistics.
