Teaching

Dedicated to educating the next generation of finance professionals

Teaching Philosophy

I believe that effective finance education combines rigorous theoretical foundations with practical applications. I aim to create engaging classroom environments where students develop both analytical skills and critical thinking abilities. My courses emphasize real-world relevance, quantitative rigor, and the importance of ethical decision-making in finance.
