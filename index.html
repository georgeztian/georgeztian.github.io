George Zhe Tian

Ph.D. in Business Administration – Finance, University of Houston (2024)

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the University of Tampa's John H. Sykes College of Business, where I teach courses in corporate finance and financial technology. My research focuses on the intersection of corporate finance, fintech, and asset pricing, with particular interest in how firms respond to environmental risks, the role of cryptocurrency as a safe-haven asset, and financial innovation more broadly.
