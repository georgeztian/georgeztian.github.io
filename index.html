Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the University of Tampa's John H. Sykes College of Business. My research interests span corporate finance, fintech, and asset pricing. I am committed to teaching excellence and providing students with rigorous training in finance fundamentals and modern financial innovations.
